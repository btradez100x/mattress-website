The idea

An ethos, not a catalogue find.

The idea for Aligna did not start in a showroom. It started on the road - years of travel as a businessman, sleeping in different cities, different beds, and waking up knowing most mattresses were a compromise.

Some were soft and gave out. Some were firm and unforgiving. Few slept cool when the climate asked for it. Fewer still felt like something you would keep for decades.

So the brief became personal: curate the mattress he wanted to sleep on every night - deep support, a cooler surface for warm markets, and a comfort layer you can refresh instead of replacing the whole bed. Aligna is that curation, made to order.
