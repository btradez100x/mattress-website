Trade and contract

Every bed in the building,
made to order.

Hotels, serviced apartments, short lets and developments. Built to your specification in any quantity, in sizes most contract suppliers do not carry, and delivered room by room.

18 sizes · One specification · Phased delivery

Why made to order

The bed fits the room,
not the other way round.

Contract mattresses are bought from stock, which means the size range is whatever the warehouse holds and the specification is whatever was made last quarter. Rooms get fitted around the mattress.

Nothing of ours is built before you order it, so a room that takes 160 by 200 gets 160 by 200.

What changes

Four things a stock supplier cannot do

01

Any size we can make

Every size we make, British through Australian. If a room takes 183 by 213, that is what goes in it.

02

One specification across the estate

Every room the same build, the same depth, the same feel. A guest moving between properties finds the same bed.

03

A comfort layer you replace, not a mattress

The top five centimetres are what wear. Renew the layer and the core stays. Across a hundred rooms that is the difference between a refurbishment and a delivery.

04

Delivered room by room

Concierged to each room, unrolled, positioned, packaging taken away. Old mattresses removed and recycled on the same visit.

The specification

One build, every room.

Thirty five centimetres. A twenty centimetre seven-zone pocket spring core with 1.8 and 2.0mm wire, 2.0mm under the hips and shoulders, a fifteen centimetre comfort section above it, and a 400 gram cool-knit cover zipped the whole way round.

The comfort layer lifts out, which is why the core carries a twenty five year guarantee and why an estate is renewed rather than replaced.

How an order works

Five stages

01

Specification

Sizes, quantities, and any variation on the standard build. We will send a sample to the property if it helps the decision.

02

Quotation

Priced against quantity, with delivery scheduled to your dates rather than ours.

Built to the order. Lead times are confirmed at quotation and held.
