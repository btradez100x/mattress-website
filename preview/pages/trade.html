Trade and contract

Every bed in the building,
made to order.

Hotels, serviced apartments, short lets and developments. Built to your specification in any quantity, in the size the room takes, and delivered room by room.

What a made-to-order supplier changes

Contract mattresses are bought from stock, which means the size range is whatever the warehouse holds and the specification is whatever was made last quarter. Rooms get fitted around the mattress.

Nothing is built before you order it. So the bed fits the room, rather than the other way round.

  1. Any size we can make Fifteen sizes as standard, British, European, American and Australian. If a room takes 160 by 200 or 183 by 213, that is what goes in it.
  2. One specification across the estate Every room the same build, the same depth, the same feel. A guest moving between properties finds the same bed.
  3. A comfort layer you replace, not a mattress The top five centimetres are what wear. Renew the layer and the core stays. On a hundred rooms that is the difference between a refurbishment and a delivery.
  4. Delivered room by room Concierged to each room, unrolled, positioned, packaging taken away. Old mattresses removed and recycled on the same visit.

How an order works

  1. Specification Sizes, quantities, and any variation on the standard build. We will send a sample to the property if it helps the decision.
  2. Quotation Priced against quantity, with delivery scheduled to your dates rather than ours.
  3. Production Built to the order. Lead times are confirmed at quotation and held.
  4. Delivery and installation Room by room, unrolled and positioned. Old mattresses removed and recycled on the same visit.
  5. Renewal Comfort layers reordered as the estate needs them, without replacing a single core.
