Why it is 200 wide

Emperor gives each sleeper a full metre. A Super King gives ninety centimetres, which is the width of a single bed. For two adults, one of whom moves, the difference is a night uninterrupted.

It is also the largest size that still takes bedding you can buy anywhere, and the largest most rooms will hold without the room becoming a bed with walls around it.

We make to order, so the size exists because someone wants it, not because a machine was already set up for it.

Built in two parts

A mattress does not wear out. The top ten centimetres wear out. Ours separates. A pocket sprung core built to last twenty five years, and a comfort layer above it that unzips and lifts off.
