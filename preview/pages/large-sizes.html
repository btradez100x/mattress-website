The size

Why it is 200 wide

Emperor gives each sleeper a full metre. A Super King gives ninety centimetres, which is the width of a single bed. For two adults, one of whom moves, the difference is a night uninterrupted.

It is also the largest size that still takes bedding you can buy anywhere, and the largest most rooms will hold without the room becoming a bed with walls around it.

We make to order, so the size exists because someone wants it, not because a machine was already set up for it.

Built in two parts

The part that softens comes out.

A mattress does not wear out. The top five centimetres wear out, and the rest goes to landfill with it.

Ours separates. A twenty centimetre seven-zone pocket sprung core built to last twenty five years, and a comfort layer above it that unzips and lifts off.

  • Depth35cm total. 20cm core, 15cm comfort above it.
  • CoreSeven-zone pocket springs, 1.8 and 2.0mm wire. Twenty five year guarantee.
  • Comfort layerRemovable and reversible. Medium one face, medium firm the other. You do not choose - it arrives with both.
  • Replacement layer£299 when it eventually softens, instead of £3,699.
  • MadeTo order, in any of fifteen sizes.
