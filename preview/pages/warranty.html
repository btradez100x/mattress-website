Years

25 years on the spring core, unless a different figure is stated on your order confirmation.

What is covered

Manufacturing defects in the pocket-spring core that affect structural performance under normal residential use.

What is not covered

Adjust to Desire is not a warranty claim. Comfort layers stay with you and are not a defect in the core.

What voids it

Misuse, commercial use without agreement, unauthorised modification, damage from improper foundations, or failure to follow care guidance.

How to claim

Email us with your order number, photos, and a short description. We assess claims in writing and confirm next steps before anything is collected.
