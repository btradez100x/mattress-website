The difference

A sealed mattress
cannot change.

If the feel is wrong at week three, a sealed mattress is wrong for ten years. If it softens at year six, the whole thing is replaced, springs and all, because the part that wore is glued to the part that did not.

Ours separates them. Twenty centimetres of springs guaranteed for twenty five years, and fifteen centimetres of comfort above it that comes out. Wrong at week three, we remake it. Soft at year six, you replace a layer for £299 and keep the bed.

The part that wears is the part that comes out.

Why you cannot choose in a showroom

Four minutes lying down
tells you nothing.

You cannot know how a mattress feels until you have slept on it for weeks. By then the decision is behind you and the money is spent, which is why most people keep a mattress they never liked.

So you do not choose a firmness when you order. Your mattress arrives, you sleep on it, and for a year you tell us how it should feel. We build a new comfort layer to that description and send it, with our compliments.

You do not choose the feel. You discover it.

Why we can do this

Because nothing is built
until you order it.

No warehouse, no stock to shift, no reason to sell you what is on the shelf. Your mattress is made after you choose it. That is why the guarantee is twenty five years and the comfort layer is remade for a year.

A year to tell us how it should feel. We remake the comfort layer to that, with our compliments, as often as it takes.
