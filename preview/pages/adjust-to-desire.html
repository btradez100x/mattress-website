Adjust to Desire

A year to decide
how it should feel.

You do not choose a firmness when you order, because nobody can answer that question in a showroom. Your mattress arrives, you sleep on it, and for a year you tell us how it should feel. We make it that way.

365

Nights to get the feel right

A hundred nights tells you how a mattress feels in one season. A year tells you how it feels in a cold January, a hot July, a week when your back is bad and a week when it is not.

That is why the number is a year. Not to give you longer to leave, but to give you long enough to know.

How it works

Four steps, and nothing
leaves your house.

01

Tell us how it should feel

Write to us in whatever words you have. Softer at the shoulder, firmer under the hip, too much give, not enough. You do not need the terminology and there is no form.

02

We build a comfort layer to it

Not a replacement of the same thing. A new layer, made to the description you gave us, in the same factory that built the mattress.

03

It arrives and you swap it

Unzip the cover, lift the old layer out, drop the new one in, zip it closed. Two minutes, no tools. The mattress stays on the bed.

04

Sleep on it, and tell us again if you need to

Once is usually enough. If it is not, we do it again. For a year, there is no limit. Each layer is with our compliments.

Nothing is sent back. Nothing is collected. The bed you are sleeping on becomes the right one.

Why a year

A bed is not one thing
across twelve months.

You will meet your mattress in four different conditions before you really know it.

Month one

It is new

Everything feels different from the bed you had before, whether it is right or not. Two weeks in, your body stops noticing and starts sleeping.

Winter

You sleep heavier

Colder rooms, more bedding, longer nights. A surface that felt right in August can feel firm in January.

Summer

You sleep hotter

Less bedding, more movement, shorter nights. The same layer behaves differently at twenty six degrees.

A bad week

Your body changes

A pulled shoulder, a late pregnancy, a month of long drives. What supported you fine in March can be wrong in October.

Why we can do this

Because the top
comes off.

Ours is built in two parts and closed with a zip that runs the whole way round. The comfort layer is made as its own product because it always had to come out again.

That is the reason a year is possible. It is not generosity. It is construction.

Afterwards

When the year ends you will have arrived at a comfort layer that is right for you. We record what it was.
