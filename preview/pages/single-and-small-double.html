Why you cannot choose in a showroom

Four minutes lying down
tells you nothing.

You cannot know how a mattress feels until you have slept on it for weeks. By then the decision is behind you and the money is spent, which is why most people keep a mattress they never liked.

So you do not choose a firmness when you order. Your mattress arrives, you sleep on it, and for a year you tell us how it should feel. We build a new comfort layer to that description and send it, with our compliments.

You do not choose the feel. You discover it.

The build

Thirty five centimetres,
in two parts.

Twenty centimetres of individually pocketed springs in seven zones, with heavier wire under the hips and shoulders. That is the part that carries a twenty five year guarantee.

Above it, fifteen centimetres of comfort closed under a zip that runs the whole way round. That is the part that comes out and renews.
