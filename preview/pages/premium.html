Three things that matter

What makes a mattress
still good in twenty years

Pocket springs, individually housed

Twenty centimetres, seven zones, two wire gauges. Each spring moves alone, so one person turning over does not move the other.

A comfort layer that comes out

The top five centimetres are what soften. Ours unzip and lift away, so the bed is renewed rather than replaced.

Made after you order it

No warehouse, no stock to clear, no last season's tension to move on. It takes weeks rather than days.

Why we can promise that

Because nothing is
built until you order.

No warehouse. No stock bought last quarter at last quarter's specification. No clearance sale, ever, because there is nothing to clear. Your mattress is wound, pocketed, layered and closed after you have chosen it, to the size you chose.

That is why the guarantee can be twenty five years, and why the comfort layer can be remade for a year. We are not managing inventory. We are making your bed.

Why you cannot choose in a showroom

Four minutes lying down
tells you nothing.

You cannot know how a mattress feels until you have slept on it for weeks. By then the decision is behind you and the money is spent, which is why most people keep a mattress they never liked.

So you do not choose a firmness when you order. Your mattress arrives, you sleep on it, and for a year you tell us how it should feel. We build a new comfort layer to that description and send it, with our compliments.

You do not choose the feel. You discover it.
