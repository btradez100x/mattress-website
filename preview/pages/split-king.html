Why two

Two people rarely want
the same bed.

One sleeps hot, one is cold. One has a bad back, one has a bad shoulder. A single mattress makes that a negotiation, and somebody loses it every night for a decade.

Two halves. Two comfort layers. Neither of you compromises.

Each side is a complete mattress with its own seven-zone pocket spring core and its own removable layer. After a month, you each tell us how your side should feel. We make two layers, to two descriptions.

How it goes together

One surface, two beds

  • LinkedJoined along the inner edge so they behave as one bed and do not drift apart.
  • One surfaceDressed with a single fitted sheet. The join sits between you, not under either of you.
  • Two answersEach layer is changed on its own. One of you can go softer without the other going with them.
  • Any staircaseEach half rolls and boxes separately, so it reaches rooms a single large mattress cannot.
