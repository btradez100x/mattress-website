Why two

Two people rarely want the same bed. One sleeps hot, one is cold. One has a bad back, one has a bad shoulder. A single mattress makes that a negotiation, and somebody loses it every night for a decade.

Two halves. Two comfort layers. Neither of you compromises.

Each side is a complete mattress with its own pocket spring core and its own removable layer. After you have slept on it, you each tell us how your side should feel. We make two layers, to two descriptions.

The join

How it goes together

  1. Two units, linked Joined along the inner edge so they behave as one bed and do not drift apart.
  2. One surface Dressed with a single fitted sheet. The join sits between you, not under either of you.
  3. Two answers Each layer is changed on its own. One of you can go softer without the other going with them.
  4. Up any staircase Each half rolls and boxes separately, so it reaches rooms a single large mattress cannot.
