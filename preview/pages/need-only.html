Two problems

Support and comfort
are not the same job.

Most mattresses solve both with the same five centimetres, which is why fixing one makes the other worse. Softer for the shoulder means less under the hip. Firmer for the back means pressure at the shoulder.

Ours separates them. The core holds. The layer adapts.

The core

Seven zones along the bed

Your hips are the heaviest part of you and they sink first. On a single-tension mattress that tips the spine out of line for eight hours, and you feel it at seven in the morning rather than at eleven at night.

Heavier two millimetre wire sits under the hips and shoulders. One point eight everywhere else. Each spring in its own pocket, so one person turning over does not move the other.
