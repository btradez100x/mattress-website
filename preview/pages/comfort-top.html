How it works

Inside our mattress

01

Unzip

Open the zip that runs around the mattress.

02

Lift out

Take the old layer out in one piece.

03

Swap in

Put the new one in. Zip it up.

On any other mattress

  1. Strip the bed.
  2. Lay the layer on the mattress.
  3. Make the bed over it with deep-fitted sheets.
  4. That is it.

It will not fix a mattress that has failed. If yours has dipped or the springs have gone, a topper masks that for a year rather than solving it.

It is 10cm deep.

On our mattress that is already accounted for - it replaces a layer of the same depth. On any other mattress it adds 10cm, which is a noticeable change in height. Your current fitted sheets may not fit. Deep-fitted sheets start at around 35cm.
