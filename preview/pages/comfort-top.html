The comfort top

Two jobs. One layer.

Inside our mattress, the comfort top is the part you replace - unzip, lift out, put a new one in, and the bed feels new again.

On any other bed, it is a 10cm topper. Deeper than most, in the same foams we build our own mattress with.

If you own our mattress

This replaces the module inside it. Unzip, lift the old one out, put the new one in.

If you do not

This sits on top of your existing mattress as a 10cm topper.

Same product either way. Choose your size and we will tell you which you need.

Which size?

Match the size of the bed it is going on. Dimensions are in centimetres.

A layer, not a mattress.

A comfort top costs GBP 250. A new mattress does not. Refresh the layer at year five and year ten and you have spent less than replacing a cheaper bed twice - and thrown away far less.

Inside our mattress

  1. Open the zip on the side of the mattress.
  2. Lift out the comfort module.
  3. Put the new one in.
  4. Close the zip.

On any other mattress

  1. Strip the bed.
  2. Lay the top on the mattress.
  3. Make the bed over it with deep-fitted sheets.
  4. That is it.

It is 10cm deep.

On our mattress that is already accounted for - it replaces a layer of the same depth. On any other mattress it adds 10cm, which is a noticeable change in height. Your current fitted sheets may not fit. Deep-fitted sheets start at around 35cm.
