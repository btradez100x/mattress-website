        Before you reserve

        Measure in centimetres. Order the size that matches.

        Bed sizes vary between manufacturers. Always trust the centimetre dimensions, not the name on the label.

        1. 01

          Measure the sleeping surface

          Strip the sheets. Measure the mattress itself - width × length - with a tape in centimetres. Do not measure the outer bed frame or headboard.

        2. 02

          Or measure the base

          If you are buying for a new bed, measure the usable bed base or slatted area (inside the rails). That is the footprint your mattress must fill.

        3. 03

          Match width × length

          Find the listed size whose cm dimensions match your measurement. Ignore marketing names if the centimetres disagree - order by the numbers.

        4. 04

          Between sizes or custom?

          If nothing lines up, use Request a size below and we will discuss options, lead time, and pricing with you on WhatsApp.
