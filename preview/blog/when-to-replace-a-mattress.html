When to Replace a Mattress (And When to Refresh the Top)

12 August 2026 · Aligna

Most beds wear out from the top down. Learn the replacement signs - and why refreshing the comfort layer can beat binning the whole mattress.

Households often replace mattresses on a calendar (“every eight years”) rather than on condition. A better approach: watch for performance failure, then decide whether you need a full replacement or a comfort refresh.

Clear signs the mattress is due

  • Sagging or body impressions that leave you rolling to a valley
  • Morning stiffness that improves when you sleep elsewhere (hotel, guest bed)
  • Noisy coils or broken foam
  • Persistent odours or allergen build-up that cleaning does not resolve
  • Heat and moisture issues that arrived as foams collapsed and airflow worsened

What wears first

Comfort layers take nightly compression. Support cores, especially well-made pocket springs, often outlast the plush top. That is why full replacement can be wasteful when only the surface has softened.

Refresh vs replace

If the design allows a replaceable comfort top, you can restore feel and hygiene at the layer that actually aged - while keeping a durable core. That aligns with a longer product life and less landfill, without pretending a tired foam top will recover on its own.

How to extend mattress life

  • Use a breathable protector
  • Rotate the mattress per manufacturer guidance (if applicable)
  • Support it on a suitable base - slats with correct spacing or a solid platform as specified
  • Address spills quickly

Buying the next one with longevity in mind

Ask how the comfort system is meant to age. Prefer transparent layer specs over vague “10-year luxury” slogans. Pair longevity with a real trial and clear refund terms.

FAQ

Is an 8-year replacement rule real?

It is a common average, not a law. Quality and use intensity vary widely.

Can I just add a topper forever?

Toppers can buy time. They do not rebuild a collapsed core. If you need deeper support, plan a proper refresh or replacement.
