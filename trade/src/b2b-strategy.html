Where the resistance is, and what to do about it.

Contract buying has gatekeepers that have nothing to do with the bed. Knowing which one you are hitting saves a quarter.

The short version

3 rules that decide where you spend the year

Owner-operated first

Independent and boutique properties where the owner or general manager decides. That is where a new supplier can win in year 1.

Flagged chains are a year 3 target

Properties under a major flag must buy from an approved vendor list. That approval is a process, not a conversation, and it is already under way.

Qualify on who signs

The first question is not the room count. It is who signs the order, and whether a brand standard sits above them.

When to walk away

4 situations, and none of them are failures

  1. The property is flagged and the bed is brand-specified

    Log the contact. Revisit when approval lands.

  2. The handover date does not fit the lead time

    Say so at the first call. A missed date costs the reference, which is worth more than the order.

  3. The budget is a contract mattress budget

    No discount closes a gap of that size. It is not the buyer for this product.

  4. They want the deposit waived

    The terms are what let us hold the price. Bring it to us rather than negotiating it yourself.
