Terms in full

Nothing here changes at the negotiating table

Minimum order

12 units on the standard specification. 25 units per size on any bespoke build, with no exceptions.

Annual commitment

None. Contract work is priced per project.

Deposit

40 percent at order on standard, 50 percent on bespoke, in cleared funds.

Balance

Cleared before dispatch. Dispatch is when the beds leave our warehouse to come to you.

Payment in full at order

A further 3 percent, on the bands noted above.

Punch-list holdback

We accept a holdback with a window agreed in writing at quote stage. It releases automatically absent a written snag.

Specification sign-off

A written specification is signed before the deposit is banked. Fire rating, sizes and quantities per size, cover, firmness, depth and delivery schedule.

Cancellation

Once production is committed the deposit stands. A bespoke build cannot be placed with anyone else.

Changes after sign-off

A change to an agreed specification is a new order, not an amendment.

Fire certification

Certificates are issued with the order for the hazard level specified.

Trade sales are final

Beds are roll packed. An opened carton cannot be re-boxed.

Quotes

Valid 30 days. Bespoke pricing is confirmed once the specification has been costed.

Price integrity

We do not discount direct, run clearance or hold flash sales. The price quoted is never undercut from our side.
