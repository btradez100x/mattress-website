One change per bed, within twelve months

You choose the firmness from a sample. Committing a whole floor to that choice is the risk, and it is the risk we take off you.

What it covers

A replacement comfort layer for every bed in the order, in a different feel, once. Not a return, not a credit. The beds stay where they are.

The window

Twelve months from delivery. Long enough to learn how the rooms actually sleep, across a full season of occupancy.

What it costs you

Nothing. It is priced into the rate, which is why the rate is what it is. A second change is quoted at cost.

Layers are kept, not swapped back. If the operator changes half a floor and leaves the rest, both feels are theirs.

How we work

Terms, in full

Minimum order

Twelve units on the standard specification. Twenty five units per size on any bespoke build, with no exceptions.

Deposit

Forty percent at order on stock, fifty percent on bespoke, in cleared funds, before production is committed.

Balance

Cleared before dispatch. Dispatch is when the beds leave our warehouse to come to you.

Specification sign-off

A written specification is signed before the deposit is banked. Fire rating, sizes and quantities per size, cover, firmness, depth and delivery schedule.

Cancellation

Once production is committed the deposit stands. A bespoke build cannot be placed with anyone else.

Changes after sign-off

A change to an agreed specification is a new order, not an amendment.

Trade sales are final

An opened roll pack cannot be resold.

Quotes

Valid thirty days. Bespoke pricing is confirmed once the specification has been costed.

Ordering

Seven steps

  1. Qualify

    What the premises charge decides fit before either side spends a week on it.

  2. Route

    The fire specification decides Route A or Route B.

  3. Specify

    Route B only. Full written specification captured and signed.

  4. Quote

    Route A from the list. Route B costed against the signed specification.

  5. Deposit

    Cleared funds. Production is committed at this point and not before.

  6. Balance

    Invoiced and cleared before dispatch.

  7. Deliver

    To site, on the agreed schedule.
