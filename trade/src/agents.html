Earned on payment

Commission is earned as each customer payment clears. A fifty percent deposit earns you your rate on that deposit straight away.

Paid monthly

Monthly in arrears, covering the prior month's cleared receipts. The statutory backstop for commercial agents is quarterly. We pay faster than that deliberately.

Clawback runs one way

If a customer refunds, the commission reverses off the next run. If a deal fails because we cannot deliver, you keep it.

What we expect

Six things

You qualify before you quote

The first question on any contract enquiry is what the premises charge. It sorts a real buyer from a tyre kicker before either side spends a week.

You quote the list price

Prices are fixed and published. You do not have discretion to discount, and you will never be asked to defend a number you did not set.

You hold the minimums

Twelve units on the standard specification, twenty five per size on bespoke. Below that it is a website order.

You capture the specification

Bespoke is never quoted in the room. You take the fire rating, sizes, quantities, cover and schedule, and it comes back costed.

You keep the pipeline visible

Stock has to be ordered ahead of what you are about to close.

You represent the brand properly

Premium furniture buyers are a small world. How you show up is the product as much as the bed is.

The honest downsides

Worth knowing before you take this on

There is no retainer

You carry your own costs from day one. If you need guaranteed income in the first quarter, this is not the right arrangement.

The first quarter is thin

Trade accounts and contract projects have long lead times. You are building in months one to three and earning from month four.

You cannot discount to close

Prices are fixed by design. If your method is to trade price for speed, you will find this frustrating.

Bespoke has real minimums

A hotel wanting ten beds in its own fire spec is not a deal we can take. You will walk away from enquiries that look like money.

You are paid when they pay

If a buyer pays late, you are paid late.

It is a new brand

No established trade reputation to trade on. You open doors on the product and on yourself.
