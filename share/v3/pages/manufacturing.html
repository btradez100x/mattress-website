The idea

An ethos, not a catalogue find.

The idea for Aligna did not start in a showroom. It started on the road - years of travel as a businessman, sleeping in different cities, different beds, and waking up knowing most mattresses were a compromise.

Some were soft and gave out. Some were firm and unforgiving. Few slept cool when the climate asked for it. Fewer still felt like something you would keep for decades.

So the brief became personal: curate the mattress he wanted to sleep on every night - deep support, a cooler surface for warm markets, and a comfort top you can refresh instead of replacing the whole bed. Aligna is that curation, made to order.

Materials

Sourced with intent

Core materials are chosen internationally - springs, foams, and textiles selected for performance and longevity, not for a single factory shortcut.

Nothing here is random. Each layer earns its place in a bed built to be refreshed, not discarded.

Assembly

Assembled in Turkey

Final assembly happens in Turkey - where skilled manufacturing brings those materials into one coherent mattress: quilted comfort, engineered support, and finishes you can see in the handle, the edge, and the cover.

Made to order for your size. Confirmed before dispatch. Built to keep.
