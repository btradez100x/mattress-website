    The Swap System

    A better bed, for life.

    Most mattresses are thrown away because one layer wore out. We made that layer the part you replace.

    How it works

    Unzip. Lift out. Swap in.

    A machine-pressed mattress is sealed for life. Ours is hand-assembled, which is why the comfort layer can be unzipped, lifted out and replaced.

    When the layer wears, replace the layer. The bed underneath has years left in it.

    To change the firmness instead, unzip the cover, turn the layer over and zip it up. Two minutes.

    A comfort layer costs around £250. A new mattress does not. Refresh the layer at year five and year ten and you have spent less than replacing a cheaper bed twice - and thrown away far less.

      Before you reserve

      Measure in centimetres. Order the size that matches.

      Bed sizes vary between manufacturers. Always trust the centimetre dimensions, not the name on the label.

      1. 01

        Measure the sleeping surface

        Strip the sheets. Measure the mattress itself - width × length - with a tape in centimetres. Do not measure the outer bed frame or headboard.

      2. 02

        Or measure the base

        If you are buying for a new bed, measure the usable bed base or slatted area (inside the rails). That is the footprint your mattress must fill.

      3. 03

        Match width × length

        Find the listed size whose cm dimensions match your measurement. Ignore marketing names if the centimetres disagree - order by the numbers.

      4. 04

        Between sizes or custom?

        If nothing lines up, use Request a size below and we will discuss options, lead time, and pricing with you on WhatsApp.
