# Facts and claims register

What is currently true, contracted and substantiated. **Owned by Ben. Reviewed whenever a supplier, carrier or certifier answers.**

This file exists because construction figures and service claims were previously kept inside the voice document, where nobody updated them. A stale row there was read as a permanent rule and led to correct copy being pulled from the site. Wording rules live in `01-BRAND-VOICE.md`. Facts live here.

**Last reviewed:** 8 September 2026

---

## 1. How to use this

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Substantiated. May be stated on the site |
| **Pending** | Not yet evidenced. Must not appear anywhere, in any form |
| **Prohibited** | Checked and false, or unevidenceable. Never state |

A `Pending` row is not a soft warning. Until it moves to `Confirmed` the claim does not go on the site, in an ad, or in a support reply.

The made-to-order exemption from statutory cancellation does not apply. It covers goods made to a customer's own specification; choosing a size from a stocked range is picking a SKU.
