The 100-night trial is offered by [Legal].

## How long
You have 100 nights from delivery to decide. That is long enough to adjust to a new bed.

## If it is not right
Contact us before the trial ends. We arrange collection. Return shipping within the mainland UAE is covered by us on the standard trial return; remote areas may differ and we will confirm before collection. UK returns: we will confirm the collection route and any carriage cost before you commit.

## Refund timing
Once the mattress is collected and inspected for damage beyond normal trial use, refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.

## Conditions
The mattress must be used with a protector; stains, tears or misuse outside normal sleep can affect eligibility. We say this plainly so there are no surprises.

## 365-night programme
A year to tailor the feel is covered by the 365-night programme. That is a complimentary comfort-layer adjustment, not this return.

## Emperor
Emperor is made to order at 200 × 200cm and is not covered by the 100-night return. The 365-night programme still applies. Your statutory cancellation rights are unaffected.
