## Lead time
Each mattress is made to order and delivered in 8 to 10 weeks. Most orders arrive sooner. If we are going to miss that window we will tell you before the date, and you can cancel for a full refund.

## How dispatch is confirmed
You receive written confirmation with a delivery window. Cancel any time before dispatch for a full refund.

## Delivery
Delivery into your home is arranged for your market. Options and any access requirements are confirmed before dispatch. There is no setup service contracted at this time.

## Areas served
UAE: mainland delivery on the founding allocation; remote areas confirmed at checkout. UK: fulfilment route confirmed per order.
