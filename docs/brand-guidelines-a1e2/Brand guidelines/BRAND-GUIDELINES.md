# Brand Guidelines

**Complete reference.** Strategy, voice, design system and components in one document. Supersedes `brand-guidelines.html`, `10-BRAND-GUIDELINES-DEV.md`, `13-BRAND-VOICE.md` and `14-COMPONENT-SPEC.md`.

---

## 0. The name is unresolved - read this first

The brand has been called Sattva, then Aligna, then Numa. **All three have conflicts.**

| Name | Conflict |
|---|---|
| Sattva | Saatva - USD 500m US luxury mattress brand, same category, same Sanskrit root |
| Aligna | Not formally cleared |
| **Numa** | **NUMA, British outdoor furniture specialist. UK distribution, premium positioning, class 20. Also NUMA Group, European hospitality operator expanding across UK cities** |

The Numa conflict is more direct than Sattva was. Identical word, same country, same trademark class.

**Therefore: the brand name is a theme setting. It is never hard-coded, anywhere.** Every document, template and asset refers to it as a variable. This has already saved two renames.

**Before the name goes on packaging, labels or a trademark filing:** commission a formal clearance search at UK IPO and EUIPO for class 20. A few hundred pounds against the cost of rebranding after launch.

**The design system below is name-agnostic.** Nothing in it changes when the name does.

# PART ONE - POSITION

## 1. What the research found

Studied: Hastens, Vispring, Savoir, Duxiana, Simba, Emma, Eight Sleep, and the 2026 furniture design landscape.

### 1.1 The category competes on heritage, and we cannot
Hastens 1852. Vispring 1901, and made the mattresses for the Titanic. Savoir created for the Savoy. Every luxury bed brand rests on the same claim: we have been doing this for a century.

We have none and cannot manufacture it. Any implied age, tradition or lineage reads as pastiche and gets caught by a buyer who researches.

### 1.2 The most successful luxury bed brand differentiates on a pattern
Hastens is recognised worldwide by its blue and white check. Retailers call it their visual signature and note the bed is designed to be seen as much as felt. Not the horsehair. Not the spring count. The check.

**In a category where every product looks identical under a sheet, an ownable visual device does more work than any specification** - and almost nobody in premium DTC uses one.

### 1.3 Warm minimalism is now the default, not a differentiator
The 2026 design press is consistent: Scandinavian-inflected minimalism is giving way to material intensity and richer palettes. Off-white, stone, restraint and whitespace is what every premium DTC brand looks like now. Being more restrained is camouflage, not difference.

### 1.4 The gap
| Territory | Owned by | Open to us |
|---|---|---|
| Heritage and lineage | Hastens, Vispring, Savoir | No, and pretending is fatal |
| Convenience and price | Simba, Emma, Otty | No, and we do not want it |
| Warm minimal aesthetic | Everyone in premium DTC | No, it is the default |
| Technology and data | Eight Sleep | Partially - we have no sensors |
| **Honest modern engineering with an ownable device** | **Nobody** | **Yes** |

## 2. Position

We are not old. We are not pretending to be. We are the mattress engineered now, for how people actually live - built to be refreshed rather than replaced, and made to order so nothing is wasted.

| Against | Their claim | Our counter |
|---|---|---|
| Hastens, Vispring, Savoir | A century of craft | A century of doing it the same way. We changed the part that wears |
| Simba, Emma, Otty | Convenience and discount | Made for you, not stacked in a warehouse |
| Eight Sleep | Technology and tracking | No subscription, no app, no sensors to fail |

**Essence:** A better bed, for life. Refresh it, do not replace it.

**Personality:** Modern, exact, quietly confident, unsentimental. We do not romanticise the past because we did not come from it.

---

## 3. The Line - the visual device

The mattress has a zip running its full perimeter, dividing the light quilted top from the dark base. It is the visible expression of the one thing no competitor offers, and it is a single clean horizontal division between two materials.

**That becomes the brand device.** Product-derived, instantly recognisable, impossible to copy without copying the product.

| Where it appears | How |
|---|---|
| Section dividers | A tonal shift at the edge, light above and dark below |
| Page structure | Sections separated by the transition, not by whitespace alone |
| Specification panel | The trigger is a full-width line between two rules |
| Photography | Compose so the product's real zip sits on a horizontal third |
| Packaging | A single band where two materials meet |
| Video | Transitions as a horizontal wipe. Never a fade, never a cut |

| Always | Never |
|---|---|
| Horizontal | Angled, curved or vertical |
| A true meeting of two tones | A rule floating on one ground |
| Hairline. 1px screen, 0.25pt print | Any heavier weight |
| One per view | Two competing in one view |
| | Coloured. It is a meeting of tones, not a stroke |

# PART TWO - VOICE

## 4. The voice in one line

**Exact, calm, unsentimental.** We state facts and let them work. We never reach for an adjective where a number will serve, and we never explain a benefit the reader has already understood.

### 4.1 Where it comes from
Every competitor sells heritage, and their voice is warm and reverent - earned, in their case. We have none and will not pretend, so the voice does the opposite job: **credibility through precision rather than lineage.** A brand that is exact about what it is does not need to be old.

Spare, not cold. The difference lives in whether the sentence is useful.

---

## 5. The three rules

### 5.1 Say the outcome. Stop.
If a sentence explains why the outcome happens, cut it. The specification panel exists for that.

| No | Yes |
|---|---|
| A 400g cool-knit cover over gel memory foam and a wave-profile layer that channels air through the mattress rather than trapping it. | Air moves through it instead of sitting under you. |

### 5.2 Short beats correct.
A fuller sentence nobody finishes is worse than a shorter one they do.

| No | Yes |
|---|---|
| Every spring is individually pocketed, so movement stays where it happens. If your partner gets up at five, reads late, or turns over twenty times a night, it does not travel across the bed to you. | Movement stays on their side of the bed. |

### 5.3 No connective tissue.
`Which is why`, `so that`, `meaning that` - the sentence is doing two jobs. Split it or cut it.

---

## 6. Conventions

| Rule |
|---|
| Sentence case everywhere except the wordmark |
| No exclamation marks. Ever |
| Numerals for all measurements and prices |
| Hyphens only. No em dashes, no en dashes |
| British English. Second person |
| `do not`, not `don't` |
| Brand name from a setting. Never hard-coded |

### 6.1 Headline formulas
| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Contrast | Change your comfort, not your mattress. |
| Flat statement | Made to order. Made to last. |
| Specification as a line | 35cm. Seven zones. One bed, for decades. |
| Counter-heritage | We did not inherit this. We engineered it. |
| The reader's own question | Firm enough? Turn it over. |

---

## 7. Never write

### 7.1 Words
| Word | Why |
|---|---|
| `premium`, `luxury` as an adjective | If it needs saying, it is not |
| `heritage`, `timeless`, `artisanal` | Borrowed from brands that earned it |
| `hotel-grade` | Borrowed authority |
| `engineered` as decoration | Fine in `engineered core`. Meaningless in `engineered support` |
| `designed for`, `built for` | Says nothing. Say what it does |
| `revolutionary`, `unbeatable`, `hurry` | Nobody believes it |
| `which is why`, `so that`, `meaning that` | Two jobs in one sentence |
| `perfect for everyone` | Nothing is |

### 7.2 Defensive writing
**Never apologise in body copy.** The honesty shows in what we do not claim, not in announcing that we are not claiming it.

| No | Yes |
|---|---|
| made for warm nights without overclaiming miracles | Heat is why people give up on a mattress they used to love. This one lets it out. |
| Most brands do not publish this | Publish it and let them notice |

### 7.3 Competitors
Never disparage the incumbents. They make fine beds. Be specific about the present instead - specificity beats nostalgia when the buyer is comparing.

Never compare our price to anyone else's, by name or by category range. Anchor on cost of ownership.

---

## 8. Claims that must never appear

| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| Country of manufacture | Only `Designed in Dubai` is permitted |
| White-glove, setup, room-of-choice, two-person delivery | Not contracted in either market |
| Any certification without its certificate number displayed | Unearned |
| Any cooling temperature, percentage or comparison | No lab substantiation. Construction may be described, performance may not be quantified |
| `Zero motion transfer` | Untrue. Pocket springs reduce it, they do not eliminate it |
| Any calculated finance figure, term or APR | Must come from the provider's own widget |
| Heritage, lineage or age implication | We are new and say so |
| Countdowns, stock counters, urgency devices | Wrong at this price |
| `Hand tufted` | It is machine tufted. Say nothing rather than claiming either |
| A second zip | There is one zip |

---

## 9. Facts that have moved

| Subject | Current truth | Language |
|---|---|---|
| **Height** | 35cm. Confirmed twice by the supplier and on the packing list | `35cm`. Still deeper than a standard UK mattress at 25 to 30cm, so the deep-sheet argument holds |
| **Wire gauge** | 1.8mm and 2.0mm | Not 1.9mm. Currently wrong on the site |
| **Tufting** | Machine tufted | Say nothing. Naming it invites a comparison with hand-tufted brands that we lose |
| **The zip** | One zip, in the cover | Unzip, turn the layer over, zip up |
| **Firmness** | Under test. Supplier rates the current build Soft to Medium Soft but reports European customers finding it Medium | Do not write firmness copy until samples are tested |
| **The flip** | Real, but a choice between two options rather than a rescue | Not `Too firm? Flip it` - the mattress is not too firm |

### 9.1 Two actions, never blurred
| Action | Words |
|---|---|
| **Flip** - change the firmness | Unzip. Turn over. Zip up. |
| **Swap** - replace when worn | Unzip. Lift out. Swap in. |

Same zip, different reason. Blurring them makes changing firmness sound like maintenance.

### 9.2 Benefit and specification
| Where | What leads |
|---|---|
| Hero | What it does for you |
| Benefits blocks | The outcome, in plain language |
| Section copy | The experience, then how it works |
| Specification panel | The number, with a short translation |
| FAQ | Whatever the question asks |

**A number appears above the specification panel only when it is the point.** 35cm is a size. 100 nights is a promise. 8 to 10 weeks is a fact someone needs. Densities, gsm and wire gauges are not.

### 9.3 The test for any sentence
1. Would a person say this out loud?
2. Is it doing one job or two?
3. Does it explain something the reader already understood?
4. Is every claim in it substantiated?
5. Could it be shorter and still true?

# PART THREE - DESIGN SYSTEM

## 10. Colour

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--ground-light` | `#F5F4F1` | Snow. Primary background |
| `--ground-soft` | `#E4E1DA` | Bone. Cards, surfaces |
| `--ground-mid` | `#3A3A3C` | Graphite. Secondary dark, muted text |
| `--ground-dark` | `#1A1A1A` | Carbon. Dark sections, headings, body text |
| `--accent` | `#8A6D3B` | Ember. Fine detail only |
| `--hair` | `#D6D2CA` | Rules on light grounds |
| `--hair-dark` | `#2E2E30` | Rules on dark grounds |

### 10.1 The complete contrast matrix
Every permitted pairing. Anything not listed is forbidden.

| Text | on Snow | on Bone | on Graphite | on Carbon |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Carbon** | 15.8 AAA | 13.3 AAA | Forbidden | Forbidden |
| **Graphite** | 10.3 AAA | 8.7 AAA | Forbidden | Forbidden |
| **Snow** | Forbidden | Forbidden | 10.3 AAA | 15.8 AAA |
| **Bone** | Forbidden | Forbidden | 8.7 AAA | 13.3 AAA |
| **Ember** | 4.4 large only | 3.7 large only | Forbidden | 3.6 large only |

**Correction to earlier guidance:** Ember on Snow was previously stated as 4.8:1 and permitted above 16px. The true ratio is **4.4:1, which fails AA for body text.**

Ember may be used only for large text - 18.66px regular or 14px bold and above - or for non-text elements needing 3:1. **Ember is never a body text colour, and never a link colour.**

### 10.2 Working pairs
| Ground | Primary text | Secondary text | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snow | Carbon | Graphite | Hair |
| Bone | Carbon | Graphite | Hair |
| Graphite | Snow | Bone | HairDark |
| Carbon | Snow | Bone | HairDark |

### 10.3 Rules
- Ember is never a fill, never a button background, never above 2% of a view
- Pages alternate light and dark grounds, roughly every third section
- Nothing is rounded. `--radius: 0`

## 11. Type

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| `--font-display` | `'Instrument Sans', system-ui, sans-serif` |
| `--font-text` | `'Inter', system-ui, sans-serif` |
| `--font-mono` | `'Geist Mono', ui-monospace, monospace` |

| Level | Desktop | Mobile | Line height | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | 64px | 38px | 1.02 | -0.045em |
| H2 | 44px | 30px | 1.10 | -0.035em |

| Label | 12px | 12px | 1.40 | +0.14em uppercase |

### 11.1 Rules
- One family. Contrast from weight and scale, never from mixing faces
- Display set tight with negative tracking. This is the typographic signature
- `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on every number
- Monospace for measurements and labels only. Never body copy
- Body never below 16px
- Never justify. Never letterspace lowercase body text
- **No serif anywhere.** It signals heritage we do not have

### 11.2 Measure
| Content | Max width |
|---|---|
| Body paragraph | 66ch |
| Lede | 56ch |
| Display heading | 20ch |
| H2 | 24ch |
| Caption, label | 48ch |

Never let a paragraph run the full width of a 1240px container.

## 12. Space and layout

```
4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 128 · 192
```
Nothing outside this scale.

| | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Columns | 12 | 4 |
| Gutter | 24px | 16px |
| Max width | 1240px | - |
| Page margin | 80px min | 20px |
| Section rhythm | 128px | 72px |

### 12.1 Breakpoints
```
sm 640px · md 860px · lg 1024px · xl 1240px
```
Mobile first. Design at 375, 860, 1240. The others are for adjustment, not new layouts.

### 12.2 Vertical rhythm
| Relationship | Space |
|---|---|
| Section to section | 128px / 72px |
| Heading to its body | 14px |
| Paragraph to paragraph | 12px |
| Body to a subheading below | 34px |
| Label to its element | 8px |
| Related items | 8px |
| Unrelated items | 24px |
| Around a hairline rule | 22px |

**Space above a heading is always larger than the space below it.** A heading belongs to what follows, not what precedes.

## 13. Motion

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--dur-fast` | 120ms | Hover, button state, small change |
| `--dur-panel` | 280ms | Panel, modal, disclosure |
| `--dur-base` | 450ms | Scroll reveal |
| `--dur-wipe` | 600ms | Section transition |

```
--ease: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)
```

**One curve. Never a second.** The theme currently contains three. Remove `--ease-luxury`, `--ease-out-expo` and `--motion-hover`.

| Rule |
|---|
| Nothing bounces, springs or overshoots |
| One movement per view |
| Stagger siblings 50ms maximum |
| Scroll reveals fire once. Never on scroll up |
| Reveal is opacity plus `translateY 20px to 0` only. No scale, blur or rotation |
| Never animate on page load. An element arriving open renders open |
| Motion is horizontal where possible. Wipes, not fades |
| No motion on price, lead time or legal terms |
| `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables everything. Panels still open, instantly |

**The principle:** every animation must answer what state change it is explaining. If the answer is "it looks nice", remove it. The feeling comes from restraint, not sophistication.

# PART FOUR - COMPONENTS

## 14. Buttons

### 14.1 Primary
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Background | Carbon |
| Text | Snow, 15px, weight 600 |
| Padding | 15px vertical, 24px horizontal |
| Min height | 48px |
| Radius | 0 |
| Width | Full width in a panel, auto inline |

| State | Change |
|---|---|
| Hover | Background `#000000`, 120ms |
| Active | Background `#000000`. No transform, no scale |
| Focus visible | 2px Ember outline, 4px offset |
| Disabled | Background Hair, text Graphite. No opacity change |
| Loading | Text replaced with a label, never a spinner. Size unchanged |

### 14.2 Secondary
Transparent background, Carbon text, 1px Carbon border. Hover inverts to Carbon background, Snow text.

### 14.3 On dark grounds
Invert. Snow background, Carbon text, hover to Bone.

### 14.4 Never
Rounded corners. Shadows. Gradients. Icons inside buttons. Scale or lift on hover. More than one primary button per view.

---

## 15. Links

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Colour | Inherits text colour |
| Decoration | Underline 1px, offset 3px |
| Underline colour | Hair on light, HairDark on dark |
| Hover | Underline becomes the text colour, 120ms |
| Focus visible | 2px Ember outline, 3px offset |

Standalone links: 15px, weight 500, underline offset 4px. No arrow, no chevron - the underline is the affordance.

**Never** Ember as a link colour. Never colour alone as the differentiator. Never remove the underline on hover.

## 19. Rules and tables

| Use | Weight | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Table rows, dividers, totals | 1px | Hair / HairDark |
| The Line between grounds | The ground change itself | No rule |
| Under an eyebrow label | 1px, 40px wide | Ember |

Never a 2px rule. Never dotted or dashed except to indicate a zip in a diagram.

### 19.1 Tables
Header 9.5px mono, +0.2em, uppercase, Graphite. Cell padding 14px vertical, 24px right, 0 left. Row border 1px Hair, none on the last. Tabular figures always. First column Carbon weight 500, others Graphite.

**No zebra striping. No vertical rules. No cell backgrounds.**

---

## 20. Focus, icons, touch

### 20.1 Focus - non-negotiable
```
outline: 2px solid Ember
outline-offset: 3px
```
Use `:focus-visible`, not `:focus`. Never `outline: none` without an equivalent. Never clipped by `overflow: hidden` on a parent.

### 20.2 Icons
Outline only, never filled. 1.5px stroke. 16px inline, 20px standalone, 24px max. Inherits text colour. Square joins. **Never alone as a control without an accessible name.**

### 20.3 Touch targets
44 by 44px minimum, achieved with padding not by enlarging the visual element. 8px minimum between adjacent targets.

## 21. Imagery

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| Colour temperature | Neutral. Never warmed to beige, never cooled to blue |
| Contrast | Higher than category norm. Detail in both extremes |
| Saturation | Low, near-neutral |
| Shadow | Deep and clean. Structure, not softness |
| Light | Directional. Raking side light reveals texture |
| Crop | Loose. Nothing important within 10% of an edge |
| Grain | None |

**Never:** showroom or retail mattress photography, beige-washed interiors, text overlays or badges, stock cliches.

**Performance:** hero never lazy-loaded, under 300KB. Others under 200KB. Explicit width and height on every image. LCP under 2.5s on mobile.

## 23. Check before shipping

1. Every text and background pair appears in the matrix in 10.1
2. Ember as text only at 18.66px regular or 14px bold and above
3. One easing curve. Grep for `cubic-bezier` returns only `0.22, 1, 0.36, 1`
4. Four durations. No hard-coded ms outside them
5. No hex outside the palette. No spacing outside the scale
6. No border radius above 2px. No box shadow anywhere
7. Every interactive element has a visible focus ring using `:focus-visible`
8. Every tappable element at least 44 by 44px
9. Form fields at least 16px
10. No paragraph exceeds 66 characters of measure
11. Space above a heading exceeds space below it
12. Tabular figures on every number
13. No serif. Nothing rounded. No spinner, gradient or hover scale
14. One primary button per view
15. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all motion
16. No em dashes or en dashes anywhere
17. No word from 7.1 and no claim from section 8 appears on the site
18. Brand name comes from a setting, never hard-coded
