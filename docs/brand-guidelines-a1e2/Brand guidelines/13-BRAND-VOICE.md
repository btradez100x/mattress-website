# Brand Voice

The single reference. Supersedes the voice sections in `brand-guidelines.html`, `10-BRAND-GUIDELINES-DEV.md` and `6-COPY-SPEC.md`.

---

## 1. The voice in one line

**Exact, calm, unsentimental.** We state facts and let them work. We never reach for an adjective where a number will serve, and we never explain a benefit the reader has already understood.

---

## 2. Where it comes from

Every competitor in premium sleep sells heritage. Hastens founded 1852. Vispring 1901. Savoir made for the Savoy. Their voice is warm, historical, reverent - and it is earned.

**We have no heritage and will not pretend to.** So the voice does the opposite job: it establishes credibility through precision rather than lineage. A brand that is exact about what it is does not need to be old.

That is why the voice is spare. Not cold - spare. There is a difference, and it lives in whether the sentence is useful.

---

## 3. The three rules

### 3.1 Say the outcome. Stop.
If a sentence explains why the outcome happens, cut it. The specification panel exists for that.

| No | Yes |
|---|---|
| A 400g cool-knit cover over gel memory foam and a wave-profile layer that channels air through the mattress rather than trapping it. | Air moves through it instead of sitting under you. |

### 3.2 Short beats correct.
A technically fuller sentence nobody finishes is worse than a shorter one they do.

| No | Yes |
|---|---|
| Every spring is individually pocketed, so movement stays where it happens. If your partner gets up at five, reads late, or turns over twenty times a night, it does not travel across the bed to you. | Movement stays on their side of the bed. |

### 3.3 No connective tissue.
`Which is why`, `so that`, `meaning that` - all signs the sentence is doing two jobs. Split it or cut it.

---

## 4. Conventions

| Rule |
|---|
| Sentence case everywhere except the wordmark |
| No exclamation marks. Ever |
| Numerals for all measurements and prices |
| Hyphens only. No em dashes, no en dashes |
| British English. Second person |
| Say `do not`, not `don't` |
| Brand name comes from a setting. Never hard-coded |

---

## 5. Headline formulas

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Contrast | Change your comfort, not your mattress. |
| Flat statement | Made to order. Made to last. |
| Specification as a line | 35cm. Seven zones. One bed, for decades. |
| Counter-heritage | We did not inherit this. We engineered it. |
| The reader's own question | Firm enough? Turn it over. |

---

## 6. Never write

### 6.1 Words

| Word | Why |
|---|---|
| `premium` | If it needs saying, it is not |
| `luxury` as an adjective | Same |
| `heritage`, `timeless`, `artisanal` | Borrowed from brands that earned it |
| `hotel-grade` | Borrowed authority |
| `engineered` as decoration | Fine in `engineered core`. Meaningless in `engineered support` |
| `designed for`, `built for` | Says nothing. Say what it does |
| `revolutionary`, `unbeatable`, `hurry` | Nobody believes it |
| `which is why`, `so that`, `meaning that` | The sentence is doing two jobs |
| `perfect for everyone` | Nothing is |

### 6.2 Defensive writing

**Never apologise in body copy.** The honesty shows in what we do not claim, not in announcing that we are not claiming it.

| No | Yes |
|---|---|
| made for warm nights without overclaiming miracles | Heat is why people give up on a mattress they used to love. This one lets it out. |
| Most brands do not publish this | Publish it and let them notice |

### 6.3 Competitor comparison

Never disparage the incumbents. They make fine beds. **Be specific about the present instead** - specificity beats nostalgia when the buyer is comparing.

Never compare our price to anyone else's, by name or by category range. Anchor on cost of ownership.

## 7. Claims that must never appear

| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| Country of manufacture | Only `Designed in Dubai` is permitted |
| White-glove, setup, room-of-choice, two-person delivery | Not contracted in either market |
| Any certification without its certificate number displayed | Unearned |
| Any cooling performance figure, temperature or percentage | No lab substantiation. Construction may be described, performance may not be quantified |
| `Zero motion transfer` | Untrue. Pocket springs reduce it, they do not eliminate it |
| Any calculated finance figure, term or APR | Must come from the provider's own widget |
| Heritage, lineage or age implication | We are new and say so |
| Countdowns, stock counters, urgency devices | Wrong at this price |
| `Hand tufted` | It is machine tufted. Say nothing rather than claiming either |
| Anything about a second zip | There is one zip |

---

## 8. Language that has changed

Facts have moved. These are the current positions.

| Subject | Current truth | Language |
|---|---|---|
| **Height** | 35cm, confirmed by the supplier twice and on the packing list | `35cm`. Still deeper than a standard UK mattress at 25 to 30cm, so the deep-sheet argument holds |
| **Wire gauge** | 1.8mm and 2.0mm | Not 1.9mm. That is currently wrong on the site |
| **Tufting** | Machine tufted | Say nothing. Machine tufting is unremarkable, and naming it invites a comparison with hand-tufted brands that we lose |
| **The zip** | One zip, around the cover | Unzip, turn the layer over, zip up. Never `a second zip`, never `its own zip` |
| **Firmness** | Under test. Supplier rates the current build Soft to Medium Soft, but reports European customers finding it Medium | Do not write firmness copy until the samples are tested |
| **The flip** | Real, but a choice between two options rather than a rescue | Not `Too firm? Flip it` - the mattress is not too firm |

## 9. Two actions, not preferences

### 9.1 The flip
The flip is described as **turning a layer over.** Never as opening, accessing, or going inside anything.

| Action | Words |
|---|---|
| Flip - change the firmness | Unzip. Turn over. Zip up. |
| Swap - replace when worn | Unzip. Lift out. Swap in. |

Same zip, different reason. Blurring them makes changing firmness sound like maintenance.

### 9.2 Benefit and specification
| Where | What leads |
|---|---|
| Hero | What it does for you |
| Benefits blocks | The outcome, in plain language |
| Section copy | The experience, then how it works |
| Specification panel | The number, with a short translation |
| FAQ | Whatever the question asks |

**A number appears above the specification panel only when it is the point.** 35cm is a size. 100 nights is a promise. 8 to 10 weeks is a fact someone needs. Densities, gsm and wire gauges are not.

---

## 10. The test for any sentence

1. Would a person say this out loud?
2. Is it doing one job or two?
3. Does it explain something the reader already understood?
4. Is every claim in it substantiated?
5. Could it be shorter and still true?

If a sentence fails any of these, it is not finished.
