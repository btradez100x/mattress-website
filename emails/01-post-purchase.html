Where your old mattress goes

Around seven million mattresses are thrown away in the UK every year and roughly three quarters go to landfill. They are hard to recycle because a mattress is steel, foam and textile bonded together, and pulling them apart takes work nobody wants to pay for.

So we pay for it. Removal is complimentary with every mattress, not because it is cheap for us but because the alternative is another one in the ground. [RECYCLING_DETAIL]
